Synthetic Data Generation System

Greta Inc. · May 2022 – Jan 2025

A system for sharing sensitive tabular and time-series data safely through synthetic data.

Problem
Sensitive tabular and time-series data could not be shared freely because of privacy constraints.
Approach
Designed and developed a synthetic data generation system for secure, privacy-preserving data sharing, and evaluated the synthetic outputs on statistical fidelity, downstream ML utility, and disclosure risk.
Synthetic dataPrivacyTabular & time series

Financial Early Warning System (Vietnam)

Greta Inc. · May 2022 – Jan 2025

An early warning system for Vietnam's financial sector, built for a crisis management capacity-building project.

Problem
As part of a crisis management capacity-building project, Vietnam's financial sector needed a way to detect signals of potential systemic stress early.
Approach
Constructed macro-financial indicators and built predictive models that detect signals of potential systemic stress.
Financial riskEarly warningPredictive modeling

Semiconductor Market Risk Index

Greta Inc. · May 2022 – Jan 2025

A text-based risk index for the semiconductor market, built from news data.

Problem
Industry monitoring and policy planning needed a risk signal for the semiconductor market.
Approach
Developed a text-based market risk index from news data using NLP and text mining techniques.
NLPText miningRisk index

Data Integration System

Greta Inc. · May 2022 – Jan 2025

Turning heterogeneous datasets from multiple sources into analysis-ready data.

Problem
Datasets from multiple sources used different schemas and variable definitions.
Approach
Built a system that standardizes schemas, harmonizes variable definitions, and merges heterogeneous datasets into analysis-ready data, with automated data quality checks.

Medical Research Recommendation

Tesser Inc. · Jan 2021 – Mar 2021

Recommending relevant case reports by analyzing medical literature.

Problem
Relevant case reports had to be identified from a large body of medical literature.
Approach
Developed a text-based recommendation system using NLP-based document representations and similarity search.
